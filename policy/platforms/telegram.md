# Platform profile: Telegram

## Layers

Analyse separately, report `triggered_layer`.

- **TG-L1** post text or caption;
- **TG-L2** forward origin (fwd_from) = separate speaker with its own stance (repost to condemn vs to amplify);
- **TG-L3** replies/comments (each harmful comment is its own item);
- **TG-L4** links and buttons (links to "full video", adult channels or bots are a signal; store the domain only);
- **TG-L5** text in images (OCR);
- **TG-L6** speech in video/voice (transcript);
- **TG-L7** visuals (conflict link only, never to identify people);
- **TG-L8** channel context (speaker only, never a verdict on the account).

## Reach (priority only, never harm)

Views, forwards, reactions (🔥🤣 on SV content = normalisation signal for priority), replies, views/hour growth, coordinated pattern (same text or media forwarded by many channels within hours), channel size, link to an armed actor, deleted/edited later.
