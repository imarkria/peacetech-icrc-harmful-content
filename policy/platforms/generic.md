# Platform profile: generic

For items with no platform-specific structure (datasets, single posts, memes). Layers follow policy/modalities.md: `text`, `image`, `embedded_text`, `meme`, `caption`, `speech`, `on_screen_text`, `sound`, `visuals`.

- **GEN-L1** the item itself (text, image, meme or video) = the only speaker unless a quote or repost is explicit.
- **GEN-L2** explicit quote or repost inside the item = a separate speaker with its own stance (as TG-L2).

Reach is unknown for these items: priority is based on content only.
